Sri Aditya Educational Society (Est. 2000) established Aditya Institute of Technology and Management in 2001 as a technical institution to impart the highest quality and standards in north coastal Andhra Pradesh under the aegis of eminent academicians, educationists, and professionals.

DIRECTOR

Aditya Institute of Technology and Management is located at K. Kotturu, Tekkali, Srikakulam District, Andhra Pradesh. It is spread over a clean, green, and serene 50-acre area. The institute has the beauty of a modern-day Gurukul. It is situated on the banks of the river Vamsadhara nearby, with the sea an hour's journey away. The institute enjoys a pleasant coastal climate. The college is marching ahead under the able guidance of Chairman Rtn (PHF) Dr. K. Someswara Rao, who is a pediatrician by profession and committed to social service with a special focus on technical education, with unstinted support from Secretary Sri. L.L. Naidu, Director Prof. V.V. Nageswara Rao, and other members of the Sri Aditya Educational Society.

Aditya Institute of Technology and Management currently offers 9 undergraduate programs in the disciplines of ECE, CSE, CSE-AIML, CSE-DS, CSC, INF, EEE, CE, and MECH. The institute also offers postgraduate programs in MBA, MCA, and M.Tech across 5 specializations. The total sanctioned annual intake is 1500 seats in B.Tech programs, 60 seats each in MBA, BBA, MCA, and BCA programs and 72 seats in M.Tech programs.

MISSION

Synergizing knowledge, technology and human resource, we impart the best quality education in Technology and Management. In the process, we make education more objective so that the efficiency for employability increases on a continued basis.

LOCATION

Located in an extent of thirty-five acres of clean, green and serene area, the institute has the beauty of a modern day Gurukul. Situated on the banks of the river Vamsadhara at a short distance and having the sea at an hour's journey, the Institute is provided with an enjoyable coastal climate. Placed in the Chennai-Kolkata NH-5 road, the institute is well connected by both bus and train travel, the nearest Railway Station being Naupada, only 5 Kms away from the campus. The institute is placed in Tekkali, which is only 45 Kms away from Srikakulam. Frequent buses are available from all sides ranging from Visakhapatnam and Ichapuram either way.
